Pocket Sweeper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Pocket Sweeper is a mobile sweeper game with optional player profiles, achievements, ranked play, leaderboards, and optional rewarded advertising. You can play in Practice Mode without creating an account. If you create an account, we collect and store the information needed to provide profiles, scores, account recovery, daily ranked-run limits, leaderboard features, Friends activity and reactions, Weekly Duo Goals, challenges, and weekly Friends competitions.

Information we collect

Pocket Sweeper may collect or store the following information when you use account, profile, or leaderboard features:

  • Account information: email address used for Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: leaderboard name, selected avatar, achievement progress, wins, losses, games played, best times, revealed tile totals, flag totals, no-flag win totals, and recent system-generated gameplay highlights used by the Friends activity feed, plus fixed emoji reactions exchanged between friends.
  • Leaderboard information: public score entries, difficulty, time, avatar, leaderboard name, and date/category information for all-time, Daily Classic, and Daily Challenge leaderboards.
  • Daily Challenge records: records that prevent repeated Daily Challenge attempts for the same difficulty and day.
  • Ranked-play records: date-based counters used to provide eight free Ranked Runs and up to two rewarded-ad bonus Ranked Runs per day.
  • Friends competition and co-op records: weekly Sweep Point totals, Weekly Duo Goal partnerships and contribution counters, Daily Challenge/Friend Challenge contribution counts, fixed activity reactions, and one-time weekly/co-op reward claims.
  • Advertising and consent information: Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform may process device, advertising, diagnostic, interaction, approximate location, and consent-related data as described by Google and based on your region and choices.
  • Local device data: offline progress, queued score submissions, settings such as haptics/sounds, theme/tile choices, and local gameplay stats stored in browser or app storage.

How we use information

We use this information to let you sign in, recover your account, submit scores, display public leaderboards, show public player profiles, provide Friends activity/reaction, Weekly Duo Goal, challenge, and weekly competition features, track achievements, enforce daily Ranked Run and Daily Challenge limits, synchronize progress, provide optional rewarded-ad benefits, and keep settings/progress available on your device.

Public information

Your leaderboard name, avatar, scores, achievements, public profile stats, and system-generated recent gameplay highlights may be visible to other players. Fixed emoji reactions are visible to the players involved in that reaction. Your email address is not shown on public leaderboards or public profiles.

Third-party services

Pocket Sweeper uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Hosting. Firebase may process technical data needed to provide authentication, database, hosting, synchronization, and security services.

Pocket Sweeper also uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to collect or display privacy choices where required. Rewarded ads are initiated by the player in exchange for a clearly described in-game benefit. Advertising availability and personalization may depend on your location, consent selections, device settings, and Google's policies. Where required, an Ad Privacy Choices control appears in the app's Settings screen.

Offline play and local storage

Practice Mode can be played offline. Signed-in players may also keep last-known Friends/profile information locally, continue an already-available Daily Challenge, and queue eligible Daily, leaderboard, weekly competition, and Duo Goal gameplay updates for synchronization when connectivity returns. Offline/local data may include settings, progress, queued wins and challenge results, cached friend/profile information, theme choices, tile choices, achievement progress, and local stats. Live account changes, fresh friend searches/requests, reward claims, leaderboards that have not been cached, and rewarded ads require an internet connection. Clearing app storage or using the in-app Reset Local Data option may remove local data from that device.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app by opening Profile → Delete Profile. The app attempts to delete your Firebase sign-in account, username claim, public profile, local saved data, and leaderboard entries associated with your account where technically available from the app.

You can also view account and data deletion instructions here: https://minesweeper-mobile.web.app/account-deletion.html

Password reset

If you use email/password sign-in, Firebase Authentication can send password reset emails to your account email.

Children’s privacy

Pocket Sweeper is intended for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at maloysius@gmail.com.
